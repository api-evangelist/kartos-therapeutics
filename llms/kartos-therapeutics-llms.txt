# Kartos Therapeutics

> Kartos Therapeutics, Inc. is a privately held, clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company in Redwood City, California, developing navtemadlin (KRT-232), an oral MDM2 inhibitor that restores p53 activity in tumours with wild-type TP53. Founded in 2016 by Wayne Rothbaum around an MDM2 asset in-licensed from Amgen, the company's lead programme is in myelofibrosis. Kartos runs no developer program and publishes no product API; the only machine-readable surface reachable without credentials is the WordPress REST content API behind kartosthera.com.

## Clinical programme

- POIESIS — Phase 3, myelofibrosis, navtemadlin added to ruxolitinib, recruiting (NCT06479135).
- KRT-232-109 — Phase 1b/2, myelofibrosis with suboptimal ruxolitinib response, active not recruiting (NCT04485260).
- BOREAS — Phase 3, JAK-inhibitor relapsed/refractory myelofibrosis, navtemadlin versus best available therapy, active not recruiting (NCT03662126).
- Reported clinical activity also in acute myeloid leukaemia and Merkel cell carcinoma. Navtemadlin targets tumours with wild-type TP53.
